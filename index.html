Model Assumptions

BDCB-informed structure: macro shocks are transmitted through macro-financial relationships to outcomes such as NPLF and CAR.
Illustrative assumptions: GDP-to-NPLF sensitivity, LGD, funding-cost effect, RWA response and non-credit losses are user-controlled and are not official BDCB coefficients.
Reference floor: 10% total CAR is used as a warning reference in this prototype.
